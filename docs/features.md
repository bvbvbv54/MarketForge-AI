- Product opportunity report.
- Market demand score.
- Competition score.
- Keyword opportunity score.

- Score explanation and recommendation.
- Competitor weakness analysis.
- Product comparison workflow.

## Listing Automation

- Marketplace-ready titles.
- SEO descriptions.

- Product image acquisition.

- PDF product report.
- Shopify-ready export.
- Amazon listing export.
- WooCommerce export.
- Cloud asset packaging.

## Business Value

- Reduces product research time.
- Makes import decisions clearer.
- Connects market research directly to content and visuals.
- Helps sellers produce better listings faster.
- Creates client-ready or investor-ready reports.
